Failed guessed repository URLs are retained as failed discovery attempts, not counted as dataset candidates.

## Acquisition decision

No legally verified, manageable, complete real annotated pair was obtained during this runtime. No access forms or emails were submitted, no mirrors were used, and no restricted scans were downloaded. Existing verified raw assets and incomplete Replica cache remain intact. Additive network requirements for `robotics.ethz.ch`, `www.acin.tuwien.ac.at` and `europe.naverlabs.com` were saved to the cloud draft while preserving existing rules; saving does not activate access. Runtime requests remain denied.

After approved access, inspect archive listing and licence before transfers, confirm a per-pair storage estimate with a 2 GiB reserve, use supported subset selection, retain archives and source URLs, verify CRC/local SHA-256 and any official checksum, then validate pairing metadata and annotations. Do not assume range support or hidden test-label availability. Full geometry-model and change benchmarks remain blocked until the required data is verified.
