How your comparison works

We prepare your frames, reconstruct each recording, and align the scenes before comparing them. Object analysis uses masks, appearance and distinct viewpoints to assess changes. Results with insufficient evidence remain Unknown.

Understanding your results

Object labels and change types are estimates from your recordings. Review the supporting views before accepting a result. An object outside the recorded views stays Unknown.

Distances use reconstruction units until metric scale is established. Real-world accuracy has not yet been benchmarked. This workspace analyzes recordings and has no robot control connection.
